Devil's Field

This short film was produced as part of my final degree project at Dongeui University. As a two-person crew consisting only of myself and a sound recordist, I handled this project from start to finish. I wrote the script entirely in Korean, found the actors, and directed them to bring my vision to life. In addition to directing, I took on cinematography, managed all equipment logistics, and editing. This intense experience demonstrates my versatility and autonomy across every stage of film production

SYNOPSIS

A university becomes the stage for a ruthless social game orchestrated by the Devil (ENTP). Sixteen students, wearing MBTI badges and armed with everyday objects, must survive shifting rules, fragile alliances, and etiquette turned into weapons. At the heart of the chaos, Heejin (ENFP) and Hajoon (ISTP), opposites in every way, are forced to cooperate, discovering that trust is proven through actions, not labels.

The Characters

ENFP, THE CAMPAIGNER

Heejin

Lead protagonist. Empathetic, creative, impulsive. Her strength lies in rallying others, even as everything falls apart.

ISTP, THE VIRTUOSO

Hajoon

Pragmatic, silent, ruthlessly efficient. Survival is his only moral code, until he meets Heejin.

ENTP, THE DEBATER

The Devil

She orchestrates the game from the shadows. She doesn't play, she watches. She stole the ISFP label to inspire trust and manipulate the Daredevils.

ENFJ, THE PROTAGONIST

Dowon

Heejin's friend. Ready to let Hajoon sacrifice himself so he and Heejin can leave the game. His loyalty has a price.
