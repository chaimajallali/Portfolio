WindMap

WindMap is a weather & wind-alert app concept: real-time alerts, wind speed timelines, and a social feed for local conditions, built around the meteorological trio of waves, wind and sea.

A university project created to explore product design end-to-end, from brand to UI.
•

The Brand

Philosophy: the logotype is built from a symbol and an original typographic mark, a wave-shaped tilde with gusts of wind, bringing a meteorological spirit into the logo itself.

Concept: Waves + Wind + Sea → Weather, location, application.
