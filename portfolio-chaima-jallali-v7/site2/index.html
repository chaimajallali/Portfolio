About Me

I'm a media & digital storyteller and creative, with one foot in web culture and the other in cinema. Backed by a double Master's degree split between France and South Korea, I bridge the gap between visual creation, data analysis, and hands-on field production.

Digital marketing and design fuel me in equal measure. I love pairing creativity with performance, whether that's analyzing campaign KPIs, shaping a brand identity and art direction (A/B testing visuals, branding projects like MadAtlas, or photography), or digging into data. I'm currently preparing for my Google Analytics certification to push my data-driven mindset even further.

When it comes to web development and mapping, I enjoy bringing ideas to life by building concrete tools from real data, like AudioMap or WindMap, interactive mapping projects I developed.

My one-year immersion in South Korea, backed by a Bachelor's degree in Korean, was a defining chapter in my journey. It gave me invaluable ground-level experience on human, cultural, and linguistic levels. Alongside that, I've built a solid background in film, working on documentary and short film sets, as well as coordinating operations for international film festivals.

Currently based in France and fully open to relocating globally, I'm ready to join ambitious teams and projects in France or internationally.
